Privacy Policy

Smart Translation AI — by X-Ray Academy · Effective date: 13 August 2026

Smart Translation AI ("the App", "we") translates technical and engineering documents. This policy explains what data we handle and why. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to provide the translation service.

1. Information we handle

  • Documents you upload. Files you upload (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text) are processed only to produce your translation. They are automatically deleted within 12 hours and are not used to train any AI model.
  • Account information (optional). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture, used only to identify your account and apply your usage allowance.
  • Anonymous usage metrics. Aggregate counts (visits, number of translations, estimated cost) used to operate and improve the service. These contain no document contents.

2. How your document text is translated

To perform the translation, the text extracted from your document is sent to a third-party AI translation provider (e.g. Google Gemini / OpenAI) solely to generate the translation, and is subject to that provider's terms. We do not sell or share your documents for any other purpose.

3. Data retention

Uploaded files and their translations are deleted automatically within 12 hours. Account data is kept while your account exists.

4. Data sharing & selling

We do not sell your personal data or documents. Data is shared only with the service providers strictly needed to run the App (hosting, authentication, AI translation).

5. Security

All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. Access controls and strict spending limits protect the service. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

6. Children

The App is intended for professional/technical use and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Your choices

You may stop using the App at any time and request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
